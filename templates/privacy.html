Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect data beyond what you actively provide through the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service — for example, to display your expense history, generate category breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third parties. We may use trusted third-party services for basic infrastructure needs, such as hosting, which may process data on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We do not integrate with advertising or tracking networks.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
